Symptoms That Mean It's Time to Call

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. Keep an eye out for the following:

  • Water leaking from the bottom or body of the tank
  • Rust flaking from the tank jacket or its connections
  • Energy bills rising while hot water gets harder to come by
  • Discolored hot water, a sign the tank is corroding inside
  • Hot water running out faster every month
  • Sediment noise that keeps getting louder
  • A tank at or past the eight-to-twelve-year mark
  • Repairs that keep coming back, one part after another

Small Symptoms, Big Consequences

Tips to Keep Your Water Heater Healthy

Most of the failures we see could have been delayed for years with basic upkeep. Put these technician-approved steps to work at home:

  • Note the install date and keep up with maintenance so the next replacement is never an emergency
  • Set the thermostat around 120 degrees to prevent scalding and reduce wear
  • Start a flushing routine in the first year, before sediment hardens
  • A fresh anode rod at the right time is the cheapest way to extend tank life
  • Keep the space around a heat pump model clear so it can draw heat from the room
  • Tankless units need a yearly descaling, especially where the water is hard
  • Make sure an expansion tank is in place if your home has a closed plumbing system
