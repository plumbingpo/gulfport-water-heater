Food Service Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and a high-temperature dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Gulfport Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule visits around your operating hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. When the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Gulfport Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
